Dining Salon Terms of Service

Terms governing table reservations, seasonal tasting menus, and culinary publications.

1. Reservation Terms & Pacing

SupperCalendar operates as an intimate communal dining salon with synchronized course service starting at 7:00 PM. We kindly ask all guests to arrive on time to ensure seamless kitchen execution across all five courses.

2. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Due to the hyper-perishable nature of our weekly micro-season farm harvests, table cancellations require at least 48 hours notice to allow seats to be offered to our waiting list.

3. Proprietary Rights & Culinary Assets

All seasonal menu blueprints, fermentation treatises, table scenography designs, and culinary photographs published on this site are the exclusive creative assets of SupperCalendar Atelier.

4. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New York, United States.
