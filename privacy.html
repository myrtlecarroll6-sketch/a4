Guest Privacy & Data Stewardship

How SupperCalendar protects guest reservation information, dietary notes, and dining preferences.

1. Commitment to Guest Confidentiality

SupperCalendar is committed to safeguarding all personal contact coordinates, dining history, and dietary preferences shared during table reservations. We do not sell, rent, or distribute guest data to commercial marketing brokers.

2. Information We Collect

When booking a seasonal tasting dinner or subscribing to our Gazette, we collect your name, email, phone number, party size, and dietary restrictions. This information is used strictly to curate your evening dining service.

3. Analytics & Telemetry

We deploy standard web telemetry (Google Analytics tag G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate page load performance, seasonal menu engagement, and reservation tool functionality without collecting sensitive guest identifiers.

4. Contacting the Data Steward

To inspect or request the deletion of your dining records, contact our Maitre d at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, or call +1-888-777-5845.
