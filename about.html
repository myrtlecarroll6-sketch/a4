Our Story

Made because a planner shouldn't fall apart by March

We're a small, independent studio — not a large stationery brand. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A planner that didn't survive one full year

Supercalendar started with a specific frustration: a paper planner that looked beautiful in January and had a cracked spine and torn pages by March. We started sourcing full-grain leather covers and heavier paper stock, testing both against real daily handling before committing to a design.

Every planner since has been built around refillable inserts, so the cover itself is meant to last for years.
