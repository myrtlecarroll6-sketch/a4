Our Story

Made because a fixed menu stops meaning anything

We're a small, independent supper table — not a large restaurant group. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A printed menu that never actually matched what was fresh

Supper Calendar started with a specific frustration: a fixed printed menu that technically listed a "catch of the day" that hadn't actually changed in months. We started planning menus the other way around — shop the market first, then decide the evening's menu around what was genuinely good that morning.

Every seating since has followed that same order: shop, then plan, then cook.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every seating

01

Market first, menu second

Every menu is planned after the morning's market run, never before.

02

One small table

A limited seating each evening so every plate gets real attention.

03

Menus retired, not repeated weekly

A menu gets one evening, then a rest before it's considered again.
